# Spare Parts and Returns Policy

This policy covers spare parts for Northwind Heating boilers bought from the Northwind Parts portal (parts.northwind-heating.example) and returns of those parts.

## Who can order spare parts

Registered installers and Authorised Service Centres order directly from the Parts portal. Northwind Heating does not sell spare parts directly to homeowners; homeowners can buy them from an authorised distributor.

## Delivery

Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days. It is free for orders over EUR 150 and costs EUR 9.90 otherwise.

Express delivery on the next working day costs EUR 15, for orders placed by 14:00.

## Warranty on spare parts

Spare parts bought separately are covered for 12 months from the date of the invoice. Parts fitted during a warranty repair of a boiler are covered for the rest of the boiler's warranty or for 6 months, whichever is longer.

## Returning unused parts

Unused parts can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are in their original sealed packaging.

1. Request a return authorisation (RMA) number on the Parts portal.
2. Write the RMA number on the outside of the parcel.
3. Send the parcel at your own cost to the address given with the RMA.

A restocking fee of 15% of the net price is deducted. The refund is issued as a credit note within 14 days of the parcel arriving at our warehouse.

## Parts that cannot be returned

These parts cannot be returned unless they are faulty:

- electrical and electronic parts, such as the main circuit board, the display board, the fan, sensors and the gas valve, once the packaging seal is broken;
- parts ordered specially for you, with a lead time of more than 10 working days;
- parts damaged during installation.

## Faulty parts

If a part is faulty on arrival, report it on the Parts portal within 8 days of delivery. Northwind Heating replaces it free of charge and pays for the return shipping. Send the faulty part back with the fault report form.

If our tests find no fault in a returned part, a test fee of EUR 35 is charged and the part is sent back to you.

If we send you the wrong part, we exchange it free of charge.

## Availability of spare parts

Northwind Heating keeps functional spare parts available for at least 10 years after a boiler model goes out of production.

## Old parts and recycling

Old circuit boards and other electronic parts can be sent back with any return for recycling under the WEEE rules, at no cost.
