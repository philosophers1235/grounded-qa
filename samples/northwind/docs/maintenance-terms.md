# Annual Maintenance Service Terms

These terms apply to maintenance plans and one-off service visits for Linea boilers, carried out by Northwind Authorised Service Centres. All prices include VAT.

## Maintenance plans

There are two plans. Both last 12 months from the date of purchase.

- Care Basic costs EUR 129 per year. It includes one annual service visit, with a flue gas analysis every second year.
- Care Plus costs EUR 189 per year, or EUR 16.50 per month by direct debit. It includes one annual service visit with a flue gas analysis every year, a breakdown visit within 24 hours on working days, free labour on repairs and a 10% discount on spare parts.

A one-off service visit without a plan costs EUR 155.

## What the annual service includes

The technician follows the Northwind service checklist:

- combustion analysis and adjustment of the gas valve if needed;
- cleaning of the burner, the primary heat exchanger and the condensate trap;
- check of the expansion vessel pre-charge, which must be 1.0 bar;
- check of the system pressure, which should be between 1.2 and 1.5 bar with the system cold;
- test of the safety devices and the gas tightness of the boiler;
- visual check of the flue and its terminal.

The service does not include repairs, spare parts, a power flush of the heating system or descaling of the domestic hot water exchanger. These are quoted separately.

## Booking a service

The Service Centre sends a reminder by email 45 days before the service is due. Please book at least 10 working days before the due date. Visits are booked in 4-hour time slots between 8:00 and 18:00, Monday to Friday.

The technician needs safe access to the boiler with at least 450 mm of free space in front of it. If the boiler cannot be reached, for example because it is boxed in, the visit counts as a missed appointment.

## Rescheduling and missed appointments

You can move an appointment free of charge up to 48 hours before it starts. Changes made later than that, and appointments where nobody is at home, are charged a fee of EUR 40.

A missed appointment that is not rebooked within the 30-day grace period means the service is late, which ends the extended warranty on the boiler.

## Service records and the warranty

After each visit the technician stamps the maintenance booklet and uploads the service report to the Northwind warranty portal within 5 working days. You receive a copy of the report by email.

Only services recorded on the portal count towards the 5-year extended warranty. If you do not receive the report within 10 working days, contact the Service Centre.

## Renewal and cancellation

Plans renew automatically for another 12 months. To cancel, give written notice at least 30 days before the renewal date.

If you cancel during a plan year and the annual service has already been carried out, there is no refund. If the service has not been carried out yet, you receive a refund for the remaining months, minus an administration fee of EUR 25.
