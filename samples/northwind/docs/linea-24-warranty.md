# Linea 24 Warranty Manual

This manual describes the warranty that Northwind Heating gives on the Linea 24 wall-hung condensing combi boiler sold in the European Union. It applies to boilers installed from 1 January 2025. Keep it together with the commissioning report and the maintenance booklet.

## Standard warranty period

The standard warranty on the Linea 24 is 24 months from the date of installation. The date of installation is the commissioning date written on the commissioning report by the installer.

During the standard warranty Northwind Heating repairs or replaces, free of charge, any part that fails because of a defect in materials or manufacture. Parts, labour and the technician's call-out are all covered.

If the boiler is installed in commercial premises, such as a shop, restaurant or office, the standard warranty is 12 months and it cannot be extended.

## Registering the boiler

The boiler must be registered within 30 days of installation. Linea Certified installers register it for you when they upload the commissioning report. Otherwise you can register it yourself at northwind-heating.example/register with the serial number and a photo of the commissioning report.

If the boiler is not registered within 30 days, the warranty runs from the date of manufacture printed on the data plate instead of the date of installation, and the boiler cannot get the extended warranty.

## Extended warranty to 5 years

The warranty can be extended to a total of 5 years (60 months) from the date of installation at no extra cost. To keep the extension, the boiler must be serviced every year by a Northwind Authorised Service Centre:

- the first annual service must be carried out within 12 months of the date of installation;
- each following service must be carried out within 12 months of the previous one;
- a service still counts as on time if it is carried out within 30 days after its due date;
- the Service Centre must record each service on the Northwind warranty portal.

Services carried out by other companies, even by qualified engineers, do not count towards the extension.

## If an annual service is missed

If a yearly service is skipped or carried out more than 30 days late, the extended warranty lapses. It cannot be reinstated, even if the boiler is serviced later.

The standard 24-month warranty is not affected by a missed service. For example, if the first annual service is missed, the boiler is still covered until 24 months after installation, but not beyond.

## What the extended warranty covers

In years 3 to 5 the extended warranty covers the same components as the standard warranty, with two differences:

- parts and labour are free, but the call-out fee of EUR 49 per visit is charged to the customer;
- wear parts are not covered after the first 24 months. Wear parts are the ignition and detection electrodes, the condensate trap seal and all gaskets.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover faults or damage caused by:

- frost, including frozen condensate pipes;
- limescale, where the water hardness is above 25 French degrees (25 °f) and no scale reducer or softener has been fitted;
- installation that does not follow the Linea 24 installation instructions or local regulations;
- corrosion or sludge in the heating system, where the system water has not been treated with an inhibitor;
- spare parts that are not original Northwind parts;
- power surges, lightning, flooding or fire;
- pipework, radiators, valves and controls not supplied by Northwind Heating.

Cosmetic damage to the casing is covered only if it is reported within 8 days of delivery.

## How to make a warranty claim

Contact a Northwind Authorised Service Centre (find the nearest one at northwind-heating.example/service) or write to support@northwind-heating.example. Have these ready:

- the serial number, printed on the data plate behind the lower front cover;
- the commissioning report;
- the maintenance booklet, if you are claiming under the extended warranty.

During the standard warranty the Service Centre aims to visit within 2 working days of the claim.

## Repairs and replaced parts

A repair under warranty does not extend or restart the warranty period. Parts fitted during a warranty repair are covered for the rest of the boiler's warranty or for 6 months, whichever is longer. Parts removed during a warranty repair become the property of Northwind Heating.

## Selling the property

The warranty, including any extension, passes to the new owner of the property if the maintenance booklet and the commissioning report are handed over. No new registration is needed.
