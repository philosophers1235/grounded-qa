# Linea 24 Installer FAQ

Answers to the questions our technical support team hears most often from installers. For anything not covered here, write to support@northwind-heating.example (Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00).

## Who is allowed to install a Linea 24?

Any gas engineer who is qualified and registered under local regulations. Installers who complete the one-day Linea Certified course can register boilers and warranties directly from the partner portal at partners.northwind-heating.example.

## How do I convert the boiler from natural gas to LPG?

The Linea 24 leaves the factory set for natural gas (G20). To convert it to LPG (G31), use conversion kit NW-KIT-31, which contains the gas diaphragm and the LPG label.

1. Isolate the gas and electrical supply and fit the diaphragm in the gas valve outlet.
2. Set parameter P02 to 1.
3. Check the CO2 value with a flue gas analyser: 10.0 to 10.5% at maximum output for G31.
4. Stick the LPG label over the gas type on the data plate and write the conversion on the commissioning report.

The conversion must be carried out by a qualified installer. A boiler converted without the kit is not covered by the warranty.

## What gas inlet pressure does the boiler need?

For natural gas (G20) the nominal inlet pressure is 20 mbar, with an allowed range of 17 to 25 mbar. For LPG (G31) it is 37 mbar. Measure it at the test point on the gas valve with the boiler running at maximum output.

## What flue lengths are allowed?

With the 60/100 coaxial flue the maximum equivalent length is 10 m. With the 80/80 twin-pipe system the maximum is 40 m, adding together the air intake and the exhaust.

Each 90° bend reduces the maximum length by 1 m and each 45° bend by 0.5 m. Horizontal sections must slope at least 3° back towards the boiler so that condensate drains into it.

## Where can the flue terminal be placed?

The terminal must be at least 300 mm from any opening window or door, measured horizontally, and at least 2 m above ground if it is next to a walkway or a public space. Fit a terminal guard if the terminal is less than 2 m above ground.

## How should the condensate pipe be run?

Use plastic pipe with an internal diameter of at least 19 mm, falling at least 3% towards the drain. External runs longer than 3 m must be insulated or increased to 32 mm to stop them freezing. Never connect the condensate pipe to a rainwater drain.

## What clearances does the boiler need?

Leave at least 5 mm on each side, 200 mm above and 200 mm below the boiler, and 450 mm in front of it for servicing. The boiler can go in a cupboard if these clearances are kept; with a room-sealed flue no extra ventilation is needed.

## How do I commission the boiler?

1. Fill the system to 1.2 bar and run the purge programme by holding the reset button for 5 seconds. The programme runs the pump for 6 minutes.
2. Check the gas inlet pressure.
3. Check the CO2 value: 8.8 to 9.4% at maximum output for natural gas.
4. Set the maximum heating output with parameter P10 to match the heat loss of the property. The range is 5.4 to 24 kW.
5. Fill in the commissioning report and register the boiler within 30 days.

## Do I need to treat the system water?

Yes. Flush the heating system before connecting a new boiler, and always flush old systems. Add a corrosion inhibitor to the system water and fit a magnetic filter on the heating return. Where the mains water hardness is above 25 °f, fit a scale reducer or softener on the cold water supply.

## Which controls are compatible?

The boiler works with the Northwind NW-RT2 OpenTherm room thermostat and with any on/off 230 V room thermostat. The NW-OS1 outdoor sensor enables weather compensation; the boiler detects it automatically when it is connected.

## Can installers claim labour for warranty repairs?

Only Authorised Service Centres are paid for warranty repairs. An installer who is not an Authorised Service Centre can claim a flat EUR 60 for a warranty repair visit only if Northwind technical support has approved the visit in advance.
